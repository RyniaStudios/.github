## ⚔️ Studio Manifesto & Philosophy

At **Rynia Studios**, we reject bloated, invasive, and subscription-hostile software. We build software as digital craftsmanship:

1. 🔒 **Local-First & Zero Tracking:** Your personal data, groceries, habits, and telemetry belong strictly to your device.
2. ⚡ **Deterministic & Ambient:** Fast, zero-lag, offline-first architectures that quietly solve everyday friction without demanding constant attention.
3. 🎨 **High-Impact Aesthetics:** Combining modern industrial titanium design, haptic tactile depth, and real-time 3D WebGL experiences.
